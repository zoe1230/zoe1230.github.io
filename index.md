## Research

My research focuses on efficient and accurate stereo matching for 3D perception. I study iterative stereo methods that preserve estimation accuracy while reducing the cost of correlation encoding and recurrent refinement, with an emphasis on real-time inference and deployment on edge hardware. I am currently interested in recovering fine structures in stereo reconstruction, particularly thin objects and sharp depth discontinuities that are often lost in low-resolution representations and coarse-to-fine processing.

- Real-time stereo matching under computation and latency constraints
- Efficient correlation encoding and iterative disparity refinement
- Fine-structure reconstruction for thin objects and depth discontinuities
